## Cross-Cutting Contracts

- `ghl.h` owns core structs, enums, EOS setup declarations, and utility
  declarations used across modules.
- `ghl_eos_functions.h` and `ghl_eos_functions_declaration.h` expose the EOS
  and HLLE function-pointer dispatch contract.
- `Doxyfile` excludes `GRHayL/include/ghl_unit_tests.h`; public test helpers
  should not be treated as user-facing docs unless that boundary changes.
- Coverage gaps above mean no direct repo-local doc/test target was found from
  the routed files; verify with deeper review before relying on indirect tests.
