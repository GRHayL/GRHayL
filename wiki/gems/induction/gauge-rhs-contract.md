# Induction Gauge RHS Contract

## Purpose

Route caller-facing audit of `ghl_calculate_phitilde_rhs` and the ET Legacy
induction gauge RHS replay. This page is about scalar-potential RHS stencil
assembly and the adjacent `A_i` gauge contribution. Interpolation and staggering
details route to
[interpolation-and-staggering-contract.md](interpolation-and-staggering-contract.md)
instead of being duplicated here.

## Public Routine

`ghl_calculate_phitilde_rhs` is declared in
[GRHayL/include/ghl_induction.h](../../../GRHayL/include/ghl_induction.h) and
implemented in
[GRHayL/Induction/calculate_phitilde_rhs.c](../../../GRHayL/Induction/calculate_phitilde_rhs.c).
It returns the densitized scalar-potential RHS from caller-assembled stencils.
GRHayL receives arrays only; no grid metadata accompanies the call.

Signature contract:

- `dxi[3]`: inverse spacings for x, y, z directions.
- `Lorenz_damping_factor`: generalized Lorenz gauge damping factor.
- `alpha_interp`: lapse interpolated to the `phitilde` location.
- `shiftx_interp[5]`, `shifty_interp[5]`, `shiftz_interp[5]`: shift stencils
  on the `phitilde` grid, ordered from two points behind through two points
  ahead in the matching direction.
- `sqrtg_Ai_stencil[3][2]`: component-major stencil for interpolated
  `sqrtg_Ai`; index `[component][0]` is the current point and
  `[component][1]` is the forward neighbor in that component direction.
- `phitilde_stencil[3][5]`: direction-major `phitilde` stencils; first index is
  x, y, or z direction and second index spans the five-point line stencil.

## Branch And Stencil Rules

Shift upwinding branches on the center element `[2]` of each shift line:
`shiftx_interp[2]`, `shifty_interp[2]`, and `shiftz_interp[2]`. Negative center
shift uses the backward-biased side of that direction's five-point stencil;
non-negative center shift uses the forward-biased side. The same center
`phitilde` value appears as `phitilde_stencil[direction][2]`.

`sqrtg_Ai_stencil[3][2]` stores current and forward-neighbor values by
component, so callers should audit assembly as:

- x component: current `sqrtg_Ai_stencil[0][0]`, forward-x neighbor
  `sqrtg_Ai_stencil[0][1]`.
- y component: current `sqrtg_Ai_stencil[1][0]`, forward-y neighbor
  `sqrtg_Ai_stencil[1][1]`.
- z component: current `sqrtg_Ai_stencil[2][0]`, forward-z neighbor
  `sqrtg_Ai_stencil[2][1]`.

The damping term uses `Lorenz_damping_factor`, `alpha_interp`, and the center
`phitilde` value, implemented as the center entry `phitilde_stencil[0][2]`.
Because the x, y, and z `phitilde_stencil` lines share the same center point
when assembled correctly, this is a caller-side consistency check.

## Relation To Interpolators

The cell-centered public interpolators under
[GRHayL/Induction/Interpolators/](../../../GRHayL/Induction/Interpolators/)
produce `alpha`, `betai[3]`, `sqrtg_Ai[3]`, and
`alpha_Phi_minus_betaj_A_j` for use here. The vertex-centered ADM interpolator
only produces `sqrtg_Ai[3]` and `alpha_Phi_minus_betaj_A_j`; vertex-centered
callers must supply lapse and shift values at the `phitilde` location by their
own route. This page only documents how valid interpolator outputs are consumed
by the gauge RHS path; stencil locations and averaging rules belong in
[interpolation-and-staggering-contract.md](interpolation-and-staggering-contract.md).

The gauge contribution to `A_i` RHS is caller-computed from finite differences
of `alpha_Phi_minus_betaj_A_j`. There is no separate public function for this
`A_i` gauge contribution; the Doxygen gauge overview and ET Legacy replay both
show it as direct caller assembly before or beside the `phitilde` RHS call.

## ET Legacy Coverage

Main visible coverage is
[Unit_Tests/unit_test_ET_Legacy_induction_gauge_rhs.c](../../../Unit_Tests/unit_test_ET_Legacy_induction_gauge_rhs.c),
with input data generated by
[Unit_Tests/data_gen/unit_test_data_ET_Legacy_induction_gauge_rhs.c](../../../Unit_Tests/data_gen/unit_test_data_ET_Legacy_induction_gauge_rhs.c).
The replay first fills interpolated arrays, then uses a narrower interior loop
for RHS assembly because that stage needs extra stencil reach in every
direction. In that loop, the test:

- computes `A_i` gauge RHS from finite differences of
  `alpha_Phi_minus_betaj_A_j`;
- assembles `sqrtg_Ai_stencil[3][2]` from current and forward neighbors;
- assembles shift and `phitilde_stencil[3][5]` line stencils over offsets
  `-2..+2`;
- calls `ghl_calculate_phitilde_rhs`.

There is no visible standalone direct unit test that isolates only
`ghl_calculate_phitilde_rhs`; coverage is through the ET Legacy induction gauge
RHS path.
